Polestar Planner — unofficial community adaptation 0.1.11 (2026-10-22)

The original Planner is by Fitz and The Polestar authors. This companion adapts
it into an in-game Lua dashboard and movable compact map with Questie overlays.
Modifications and public packaging: AurumTitanos, 2026. GPL-3.0-only; see LICENSE.
Polestar, guide progress and optional Questie remain separately installed.

OpenMap code/data: jiforever and OpenMap contributors, MIT, from mirror
fitzforge/wowforever-openmap commit 849bac460b6ebcb110ef80fc27a6b08565069c74.
Original notices are in OpenMap/LICENSE.txt and OpenMap/NOTICE.md.
Maps/RouteStrokes.lua retains Cillian Berragan's Shortest Path Forever notice.

No game artwork, UI media, fonts, guide content, personal macros or character
saves are shipped. Client textures are referenced by ID; installed Polestar
supplies UI media. OpenMap supplies its own optional stitched artwork. An
optional local mask generator is included in the public source repository.
Full credits, corresponding source and policy notes:
https://github.com/AurumTitanos/Aurums-Polestar-Planner
